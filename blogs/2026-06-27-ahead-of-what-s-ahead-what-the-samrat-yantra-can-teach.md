What the Samrat Yantra Can Teach Modern Enterprises About the Future Centuries before algorithms, dashboards, predictive analytics, or artificial intelligence, India built something extraordinary. The Samrat Yantra, the world’s largest stone sundial, was never built simply to tell time. It was built to understand movement, measure change, and create precision from the unknown.
